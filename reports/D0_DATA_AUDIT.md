# D0 data audit (DrugWorld)

Generated by `scripts/01_audit.py` from the IDR annotation repositories (`IDR/idr0119-gross-cellresponse` at `2db16f9`, `IDR/idr0064-goglia-erkdynamics` at `cae51ac`), the 34 idr0119 companion OME-XML files, and the image records. Numbers are counted from those files; text marked *source* is quoted or paraphrased from the study files or the papers.

## idr0119 (Gross et al., Nat Commun 2023)

- Licence on the record: CC BY 4.0 (`idr0119-study.txt`).
- 34 plates, 760 wells, 3026 well fields (movies); fields per well: {3: 14, 4: 746}.
- 1,748,979 single-plane TIFFs (uint16, 1408 x 1040), 3 channels: R = nuclear reporter (NLS-mCherry), G = cell-cycle reporter (clover-HDHB), P = phase contrast. **The reporter is HDHB, not FUCCI**: G1 vs S/G2 is read from HDHB localisation (nuclear/cytoplasmic ratio), as in the study's feature files.
- Frames: every 30 min from 0 to 96 h (193 slots) on every plate, taken from the file-name time stamps. The study text says the three non-AU565 lines were imaged every 2 h; the deposited files of all 34 plates are at 30 min, so the files are used.
- Fields with all 193 slots: 7731 of 9078 (field x channel); the rest miss 1-7 slots (listed in `results/d0/audit.json`).
- **Dose units:** the library file labels compound 1 concentrations as micromolar and compound 2 as nanomolar. The paper states "All doses in nM" and its quoted doses (e.g. 50 nM lapatinib, 3 nM paclitaxel, 30 nM gemcitabine) match the compound 1 values, so **both columns are nM**. The plan's dose encoding (log10 of dose / the drug's maximum grid dose) does not depend on the unit.
- **Timing of drug addition** (*source*: paper methods, "After drug addition (T0), plates were imaged every 2 h [AU565: 30 min] for 96H"): the first frame is at drug addition, so drug addition precedes the first frame by <= 1 h for every plate. Under the frozen start-state rule the well's own first clip is the start state.
- *Source* (study protocol): for AU565 single-drug plates, "at 48H the media was replaced in all wells including the control wells, and fresh media and drug were added". This applies to AU565 single drugs only and is recorded here for the protocol (re-dosing at 48 h, between the 24 h and 72 h horizons).
- *Source*: "Four equally-spaced image locations per well and three biological replicates were collected."

## idr0064 (Goglia et al., Cell Syst 2020)

- Licence on the record: CC BY 4.0 (`idr0064-study.txt`).
- 9 plates (384-well), 450 imaged wells, one field per well.
- 428 compounds, all at [2.5] uM (one dose). Wells per compound: {1: 427, 2: 1} (count of compounds by number of wells): **each compound is imaged in a single well, so there are no replicate wells per compound.**
- DMSO vehicle wells per plate: {1.1: 2, 1.2: 2, 1.3: 2, 2.1: 2, 2.2: 2, 2.3: 2, 3.1: 2, 3.2: 2, 3.3: 2}; untreated wells: 3.
- Images: 512 x 512 px at 1.6 um, 2 channels (ErkKTR-BFP (405), H2B-RFP (561)), 121 frames at 3.0 min (ImageJ `finterval` in the TIFF header), so 6.0 h per well; one multi-frame TIFF per well (127 MB).
- **Timing of drug addition** (*source*: study protocol, "Imaging was carried out 30 min after drug additions"): 30 min before the first frame, i.e. <= 1 h, so the well's own first clip is the start state under the frozen rule. The study text says intervals of 2-3 min across the paper's experiments; the screen files record 3 min.
- Target classes (vendor annotation, compounds): PI3K/Akt/mTOR 95, Protein Tyrosine Kinase 87, MAPK 45, Others 45, Angiogenesis 44, Cell Cycle 44, JAK/STAT 25, Neuronal Signaling 7, NF-κB 7, DNA Damage 6, GPCR & G Protein 4, Cytoskeletal Signaling 4, TGF-beta/Smad 4, Autophagy 4, Epigenetics 3, Metabolism 2, Microbiology 1, Endocrinology & Hormones 1, Transmembrane Transporters 1.
- Structures: the library file gives the CAS number and the PubChem URL (CID) per compound, no SMILES; SMILES will be taken from PubChem by CID (source recorded per compound).
- Goglia's Erk-phenotype class per compound (needed for the scaffold-split stratification): not in the IDR files; to be taken from the paper's supplementary table in D2.

## Points for the author

1. idr0119's reporter is HDHB (not FUCCI); channel mapping (ii) uses R = NLS-mCherry, G = clover-HDHB, B = phase.
2. idr0064 has one well per compound; the H2b unit (the compound) therefore has one well, and "errors averaged over replicate wells" averages over one well.
3. AU565 single-drug wells were re-dosed at 48 h.
