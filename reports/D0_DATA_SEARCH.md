# D0.0 data expansion search (DrugWorld)

- **Date:** 2026-10-30
- **Scope:** read-only search of public metadata pages and APIs. No image data was downloaded. The only data files fetched were small metadata/annotation files: IDR annotation CSVs (<1 MB each), BioImage Archive file-list JSON, and the 24 MB of cell-count CSVs in Zenodo 18292967. Nothing was committed, posted or uploaded.

## Criteria (all four must hold)

1. At least 50 small-molecule compounds, each at 3 or more doses.
2. At least 12 h of time-lapse imaging, meaning repeated frames of the same live wells/fields. Fixed-cell endpoint screens (for example Cell Painting snapshots) do not qualify unless the same live cells are imaged repeatedly for 12 h or more.
3. Replicate wells.
4. Open licence (CC0, CC BY, MIT/BSD-style). NC or ND licences fail this criterion.

## Notes per candidate

1. **idr0119.** The annotation CSV has 760 wells and 7 distinct compounds (single agents plus combinations in AU565). Single-agent dose counts per drug range from 7 (lapatinib) to 18 (gemcitabine). Replicates per (line, drug, dose) are mostly 3: 111 of 166 groups have 3 wells, 4 groups have 1. The licence on idr0119-study.txt is "CC BY 4.0". It meets every criterion except the compound count (7, against a threshold of 50).
2. **idr0064.** Every one of the 450 annotated wells is at 2.5 µM. The study description says "monitoring Erk activity over 5 h". It fails the dose, duration and replicate criteria.
3. **idr0139 screenC.** This is the closest IDR match. The screen description says "a representative panel of 52 compounds ... at a range of concentrations ... over 72" h, and the Screen Comments list 18 timepoints from 4 h to 72 h. My tally of `screenC/idr0139-screenC-annotation.csv` gives 1,527 wells, 4 plates (two per cell line), 52 compounds with 12 concentrations each (0.00003, 0.0003, 0.001, 0.003, 0.0125, 0.03, 0.1, 0.3, 1.25, 3.125, 10, 30 µM), and exactly one well per (compound, concentration, cell line). Controls: 126 DMSO, 96 negative, 63 stimulator (leptomycin B). It therefore fails the replicate-well criterion. Two further caveats for DrugWorld:
   - The compounds are given only as proprietary "SN..." IDs with a target gene symbol (e.g. SN1151683253 → JAK2), with no name or SMILES.
   - The 4 h frame interval and single field per well are coarse compared with idr0119.

   The two cell lines (fascin WT re-expression vs KD) are not replicates of each other.
4. **EUbOPEN live-cell multiplex.** The study type is "compound library screen" and the description is "high-content live-cell multiplex screen". I tallied the file-list metadata, which has per-file `concentration_uM`, `timepoint_in_hours`, `biological_duplicate` and `well_name` fields:
   - Every CQ1 wave uses exactly two concentrations (1 and 10 µM), duplicate wells, and three cell lines.
   - The same wells are re-imaged at 0/12/24/48 h, so each well has 3 to 4 timepoints. That is repeated live imaging, but very sparse.
   - The wave 1 Incucyte component (566 compounds at 10 µM, 0 to 24 h) has one dose.

   Licence "CC0" appears on the newer records only. It fails the 3-dose criterion in every wave.
5. **S-BIAD2515.** A single compound over 6 h. The licence is CC0. It fails C1 and C2.
6. **Zenodo 18292967.** This is numerically the closest match: 137 drugs x 7 doses x 2 wells x about 120 h of repeated imaging of the same 384-well plates. However, the record contains only per-well nuclei counts ("cell.count"), not images. The description says the data "were originally hosted on the Thunor web application ... that has since gone offline". The record also has a defect: HTS003.csv and HTS031.csv have the same MD5 (973b12b7...) and both contain the SCLC (HTS031) data. It does not qualify as an imaging dataset. It could only serve as a non-image cell-count side resource, which is outside the DrugWorld design.
7. **HMS LINCS Mills 2015.** The live-cell readout is at a single time ("imaged live following 24 hours"). The page lists no open licence.
8. and 9. **LINCS MCF10A sets.** Too few small molecules (8), or ligands rather than small molecules.
10. and 11. **Cell Painting Gallery.** The registry licence is "CC0 1.0 Universal". The only "live" subset is cpg0039 (silver nanoparticles, Huh7). cpg0029-chroma-pilot is a dye comparison. cpg0045-ncats-mito exists on S3 but has no README entry, so I did not evaluate it further. No time-lapse compound subset was found.
12. to 21. These are single- or few-compound studies, non-cell systems, non-image data, or NC-licensed. Each fails at least one criterion, as shown in the table.

**IDR coverage.** Across all 217 rows of studies.tsv, the only screens with T > 1 are idr0002, idr0013 (RNAi), idr0061 (RNAi), idr0064, idr0092 (organoids, T=5), idr0119 and idr0139 screenC. Caveat: IDR stores some time series as separate images, so a T=1 entry does not completely rule out time-lapse. However, the compound screens with T=1 that I inspected describe fixed endpoints (e.g. idr0139 screens A/B, idr0140, idr0145, idr0160, idr0171).

## Conclusion

**No dataset meets all four criteria; proceed with idr0119 and idr0064 as designed.**

Near misses, recorded so they can be revisited. None is adopted, and each fails at least one criterion on verified facts:
- **idr0139 screenC.** Passes compounds (52 x 12 doses), time-lapse (72 h at 4 h) and licence (CC BY 4.0). **Fails on replicate wells**: one well per compound, dose and cell line. The compound identities are also proprietary.
- **EUbOPEN live-cell multiplex (S-BIAD145 and related).** Passes compounds, replicates and licence (CC0 on the newer waves). **Fails on doses**: only 2 concentrations. Re-imaging is sparse (0/12/24/48 h).
- **Zenodo 18292967.** Passes all four as numbers (137 drugs x 7 doses x 2 wells, about 120 h, CC BY 4.0), but **has no images** (cell counts only).

No candidate is left as "unclear" on all four criteria. The rows marked "unknown" in single fields are already disqualified by another verified field.
